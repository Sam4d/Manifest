      FROM FOLDER TO FRESH START

      Your website’s next chapter
      starts with a clearer map.

      Three simple steps. No uploads, no accounts, and no changes to your original files.

      01

      Choose your website

      Click Choose website folder and select the extracted root folder containing your HTML, CSS, scripts, and assets. ZIP files need to be extracted first.

      Set the binary cutoff first. 5 MiB is a good starting point.
      02

      Find what matters

      Review the space breakdown, explore Pages & structure, and inspect Files & assets. “Keep” means copied. “Omit” means inventoried but left out.

      Layouts are inferred from HTML, not screenshots or runtime behavior.
      03

      Export & make it yours

      Click Export blueprint and choose a folder outside the website. A new, uniquely named folder contains the report, manifest, source, and rebuilding brief.

      Give your AI the manifest and brief. Add your own media and branding.

      Meet your export

      report.html

      Open in a browser for a readable overview of the structure and decisions.

      manifest.json

      Use with an AI to understand content, components, dependencies, and file references.

      lightweight/

      Source and smaller assets. Media is replaced where supported; CSS and JavaScript references may need editing.

      REBUILD.md

      A handoff brief with reconstruction guidance and known limitations.

      A blueprint, not a pixel-perfect clone. SiteAtlas doesn’t execute scripts, compile the website, or discover every dynamic route. Review source for embedded secrets before sharing it. Media placeholders may change the layout.
